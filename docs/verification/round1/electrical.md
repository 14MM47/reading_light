# Electrical design review: Moon reading light, main board + LED board (rev 0.1)

Reviewer: independent electrical review, 2026-11-02. No repo files were edited.

## Scope and inputs

- **Sources:** `hardware/main-board/design.py`, `hardware/led-board/design.py`, `hardware/parts.py`, `hardware/lib/make_lib.py` and `hardware/lib/make_footprints.py`.
- **Generated files:** `*.kicad_sch`, `*.kicad_pcb` and `*.kicad_pro`, plus `reports/*.net` and `reports/{erc,drc,route}.json`.
- **Firmware** (pin and address assumptions only): `firmware/include/{config.h,board_map.h}`, `firmware/src/main.cpp`, `firmware/platformio.ini` and `firmware/lib/core/lightcore.h`.
- **Datasheets:** `.../scratchpad/ds/*.txt|pdf`. Diagrams were checked against page renders, not only the pdftotext output.
- **Downloaded for this review:** Vishay S1A–S1M, Doc. 88711, rev. 07-May-2024, from `https://www.vishay.com/doc?88711`, saved at `.../scratchpad/dl_s1j/s1a.pdf`.
- **Not obtained:**
  - Littelfuse SMF5.0A datasheet. littelfuse.com returns 403, and this turn's web-search budget was used up.
  - Samsung DC-bias data for CL21A106KOQNNNE, the 0805 10 µF part actually fitted.
  - The battery's PCM and cell datasheets. Any figure that depends on these is marked **UNVERIFIED** or **ASSUMED**.

Scripted checks (in `.../scratchpad/scripts/`):

- `parity.py`: design.py pin→net, then the netlist, then the PCB pad nets. Also value, footprint and every field (MPN, Farnell and so on) against `parts.py`. **0 mismatches on both boards.** The generated files are current with the sources.
- `fpcmp.py`: every placed footprint compared with its library `.kicad_mod` (pad numbers, positions, sizes and types). **All 56 are identical.**

Severity scale:
- **BLOCKER:** the board won't work, or it is unsafe.
- **MAJOR:** it works badly or is out of spec.
- **MINOR**
- **NOTE**

## Power-path verification (all states)

The P-FETs as built are `Q_PMOS_GSD` on the KiCad SOT-23 footprint: pad 1 = G, pad 2 = S, pad 3 = D. That matches the Diodes DMG2305UX top view: G bottom-left, S bottom-right, D top. The body diode runs D→S (anode at drain), confirmed on the p1 render.

| FET | G | S | D | Body diode | Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q3 | Q3_G (R10 10 k → GND) | BAT | BAT_RAW (J2.1) | BAT_RAW → BAT | reverse-battery (correct orientation) |
| Q1 | VBUS (R5 100 k → GND) | VSYS_RAW | BAT | BAT → VSYS_RAW | AN1149 load share (correct orientation) |
| Q2 | SW_GATE (R6 1 M to S, C5 100 n G–S, R7 10 k to SW1 common) | VSYS_RAW | VSYS | VSYS → VSYS_RAW | load switch (correct orientation) |

D3 S1J: pad 1 = K = VSYS_RAW, pad 2 = A = VBUS. Correct.

| State | Q3 | Q1 | Q2 | Result / Vgs check |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Battery only, switch ON | ON (Vgs = −VBAT, −3.0…−4.2 V) | ON (Vgs = −VSYS_RAW) | ON (gate ≈ VSYS_RAW × 10k/1.01M ≈ 40 mV) | VSYS ≈ VBAT − I × (3 × ~45 mΩ). All \|Vgs\| ≤ 4.2 V, within ±8 V. |
| Battery only, switch OFF | ON | ON | OFF (G = S = VSYS_RAW via R7 and R6, no current) | VSYS isolated. **VSYS_RAW and BAT stay live.** Standby budget below. |
| USB only, switch ON | ON (charger output drives BAT_RAW, no cell) | OFF (Vgs = VBUS − VSYS_RAW ≈ +Vf(D3)) | ON | VSYS = VBUS − Vf(D3) − ε: ≈ 4.3 V light load, ≈ 4.1 V at 0.4 A (S1J ≈ 0.88 V at 0.4 A). |
| USB + battery, switch ON | ON (charge current S→D) | OFF | ON | Load fed from VBUS through D3. The charger sees only the cell, so termination works (AN1149). The body diode conducts only if VSYS_RAW < BAT − ~0.5 V (weak USB, see M4). |
| USB, switch OFF | ON | OFF | OFF | Charges normally. VSYS ≈ 0. No MCU pin is driven: pull-ups go to VSYS, the LED board is on VSYS, buttons go to GND. **No back-powering.** Q1 Vgs ≈ +0.7 V. |
| Reversed battery, no USB | OFF (Vgs ≥ 0; body diode reverse-biased, IDSS ≤ 1 µA) | — | — | Blocked. BAT_RAW is reached only by Q3.D and TP2. |
| Reversed battery + USB | partly ON | OFF | — | About 42 mA flows (see m2). |
| USB unplug under load | ON | ON after ~0.3–0.4 s (see m1) | ON | Body-diode handover dip. |
| Programmer powering J4.1 | ON | ON | body diode or ON | Back-charges the cell (see M3). |

**Inrush (Q2):** about 1 A peak for ~0.1 ms. Acceptable (N1).

**Battery hot-plug with the switch ON:** C5 drags the gate up together with the source, so Q2 starts OFF and then soft-starts. Good.

### Switch-OFF battery drain (battery only)

| Leakage path | Datasheet basis | Worst case | Typical |
|---|---|---|---|
| U1 VBAT reverse leakage (VDD < VSTOP; R5 holds VDD at 0) | `mcp73831.txt` l.228: 0.15 typ / 2 µA max | 2 µA | 0.15 µA |
| Q2 IDSS (VSYS_RAW→VSYS, VDS ≈ −4 V) | DMG2305UX l.111: ≤ 1 µA @ −20 V | ≤ 1 µA | ~nA |
| D3 reverse (VSYS_RAW→VBUS→R5) | S1J IR ≤ 1 µA @ 600 V; Fig. 4 shows ~0.004 µA @ 10 % VRRM | ≤ 1 µA | < 0.01 µA |
| C2 and C3 IR (10 µF each at 4.2 V) | Samsung spec ≥ 100 MΩ·µF, i.e. 10 MΩ (ASSUMED the same for the 0805 part) | 0.84 µA | nA |
| Q1 and Q3 gate leakage (through R5/R10) | IGSS ±100 nA @ 8 V | 0.2 µA | nA |
| R6/R7/C5 | 0 V across them in OFF | 0 | 0 |

**Total: ≤ ~5 µA worst case, about 0.2–0.3 µA typical.** That is negligible: the pack's own PCM (typically a few µA) dominates. The TVS leakage is not involved, because VBUS stays at about 0 V.
